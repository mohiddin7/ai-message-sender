Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated 2026-08-27. Short version: nothing leaves your browser unless you turn on cross-device sync. Works on any webpage with a text input and a send button — not limited to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

What we store

All data — your queued messages, taught selectors, and settings — is stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local. Selectors and settings may be synced across your devices via chrome.storage.sync, which is end-to-end your Google account and never seen by us.

  • Your queue and history (last 30 days, capped).
  • Recurring rules you create.
  • CSS selectors you teach the picker.
  • Onboarded flag and UI preferences.

What we do not do

The extension code makes no network requests. The Chrome Web Store listing itself does not see your data.

  • We do not make any network requests from the extension code.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
  • We do not read your past conversation history with any AI platform.
  • We do not sell or share any data — there is no "we" receiving it.

Limitations

Scheduled sends depend on your browser, not a server — a few real constraints follow from that.

  • The tab you scheduled a message in must stay open. Closing it cancels that send — you'll see it marked "tab lost" in History.
  • Chrome itself must be running. If you fully quit Chrome, queued sends fire late once you reopen it, not retroactively at the original time.
  • Your device just needs to be awake — a locked screen is fine, sleep or hibernate is not.

What you can do

Open the Options page to wipe all stored data, clear synced selectors, and reset the tutorial state.

Use the Options page → Maintenance section → Wipe local data and Clear synced selectors to reset everything. Uninstalling the extension also removes all stored data.
